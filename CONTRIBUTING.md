# Contributing to Proof of Reciprocity

This research brief is open for critique, refinement, counterexamples and design proposals.

Start with the [main discussion issue](https://github.com/zmobiezmo/proof-of-reciprocity/issues/1), or select a passage on the website and choose **Quote selection to GitHub**.

Please distinguish **verified evidence**, **reported experience**, **interpretation**, and **hypothesis**. Show sources where available. We encourage arguments *against* the proposal and tests of unintended harm.

The project does not endorse compulsory worldwide social scoring. Proposals should address: consent, privacy, context, informed participation, correction, challenge/appeal, rehabilitation, anti-Sybil measures, and resistance to capture.

Avoid identifying or accusing private people. Do not submit private logs, passwords, or sensitive personal information.
