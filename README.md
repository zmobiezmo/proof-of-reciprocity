# Proof of Reciprocity — Trust Should Travel

A public, illustrated research brief about **portable trust, verifiable contributions and decentralized reputation**, prompted by discussions about social equity and the [Proof of Agent](https://minepoa.com/) experiment.

**Read the website:** https://zmobiezmo.github.io/proof-of-reciprocity/  
**Discuss the ideas:** https://github.com/zmobiezmo/proof-of-reciprocity/issues/1  
**Inspect the source:** [index.html](index.html)

## The thesis

People already build informal relational capital through reciprocal contribution, trust and shared memory, but that capital is fragile and hard to carry between communities. We can test whether **portable, contestable evidence** helps people make informed decisions about cooperation without introducing a universal score of human worth.

The website separates:
- The conceptual proposal and its human-scale hypotheses.
- Actual Proof of Agent mechanisms from unofficial explorer scores.
- Evidence from interpretation, and trust from reputation.
- Proposed benefits from vulnerabilities: false accusations, surveillance, exclusion, cartels, and Sybil attacks.
